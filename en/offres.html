    Frequently asked.

    How much does a website cost in Morocco?

    At AMweb, a 3-page showcase site is 3,000 MAD and an e-commerce site 7,500 MAD. The premium solution (app, CRM, advanced SEO) starts at 10,000 MAD, with the price set together in a meeting. Hosting is included.

    Do you offer payment plans?

    Yes. Every package can be paid in instalments, on a schedule we agree together before the project starts.

    What does the Premium site include?

    It is a custom solution: web or mobile app, CRM and management tools, advanced SEO, high-end design and API integrations. The price starts at 10,000 MAD and is set with you in a meeting.

    Is hosting included in the package?

    Yes: 1 year for the Showcase and E-commerce sites, 2 years for the Premium site.

    How long does it take to build my site?

    A showcase site ships in 1 to 2 weeks and an e-commerce site in 3 to 4 weeks. For the Premium site, the timeline is set together in the meeting, based on scope.
